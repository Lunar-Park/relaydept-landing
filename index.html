Production / Relay Dept

From concept to
creator-ready delivery

Relay Dept is a principal-led video production practice for projects that need one accountable owner from concept through delivery.

// one accountable lead / concept → delivery

Capabilities

01

Creative Development

Treatments, decks, budgets, and capture plans that turn a brief into a shootable project before anyone walks on set.

02

Physical Production

Crew, vendors, locations, builds, safety, schedule, and set execution — owned by one production lead.

03

Post-Production

Edit, color, sound, graphics, finishing, and approvals kept connected through final delivery.

04

Creator-Ready Delivery

A hero film plus versions, stills, captions, and a usable bank of social moments — specified before the shoot.

Relay owns the production lane. For agency-led work, we work under the agency's paper and leave the client relationship with the agency; media buying, distribution, and account management are separate scopes.

How we work

  1. 01 — Pre-production

    Pre-production

    Brief, treatment, scope, schedule, budget, crew, locations, and approvals. The decisions that protect the shoot happen before spend.

  2. 02 — Production

    Production

    One production lead owns the day — crew, vendors, safety, schedule, and changes stay connected to the brief.

  3. 03 — Post

    Post

    Selects, edit, color, sound, graphics, finishing, and revisions move through one accountable workflow.

  4. 04 — Delivery

    Delivery

    Hero film, versions, stills, captions, and creator-ready moments arrive as one defined asset package.
